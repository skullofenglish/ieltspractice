IELTS Speaking Part 1: Introduction & Interview

General questions about yourself, your home, work, studies, and familiar topics.

Sample Questions:

    Where do you live? Do you live in a house or an apartment?

    What do you like most about your hometown?

    Let's talk about your daily routine. What time do you usually get up?

    Do you prefer mornings or evenings? Why?

Dica: Pratique respondendo com frases naturais, desenvolvendo um pouco a resposta sem exagerar no tempo.

IELTS Speaking Part 2: Long Turn (Cue Card)

Talk on a specific topic for 1 to 2 minutes after 1 minute of preparation.

Describe a book that you read recently.

You should say:

  • what kind of book it is
  • what it is about
  • why you decided to read it

and explain what you learned from reading this book.

IELTS Speaking Part 3: Two-way Discussion

More abstract and deeper discussion related to the topic in Part 2.

Discussion Topics (Reading & Literature):

    What kinds of books are most popular in your country today?

    Do you think reading books in digital format will ever completely replace traditional paper books?

    Why do you think some people prefer reading physical books rather than e-books?
