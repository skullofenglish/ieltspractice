IELTS Speaking Practice

Part 1 - Introduction and Interview (4-5 minutes)

The examiner will ask you general questions about yourself and a range of familiar topics, such as home, family, work, studies and interests[cite: 7, 23].

Exemplo de Tópicos Comuns:

  • Where do you live? Do you live in a house or an apartment?
  • What do you like about your hometown?
  • Do you work or are you a student? What subjects are you studying?
  • Let's talk about free time. What do you usually do at weekends?

IELTS Speaking Practice

Part 2 - Long Turn / Cue Card (3-4 minutes)

You will be given a task card with a topic. You have 1 minute to prepare and make notes. Then you must speak for 1 to 2 minutes.

Describe a book that you recently read.

You should say:

  • what kind of book it is
  • what it is about
  • why you decided to read it
  • and explain how you felt about it.

IELTS Speaking Practice

Part 3 - Two-way Discussion (4-5 minutes)

The examiner will ask further questions connected to the topic in Part 2. These questions give you an opportunity to discuss more abstract ideas and issues[cite: 7, 23].

Exemplo de Questões de Discussão:

  • What are the differences between reading books in print and reading them digitally?
  • Do you think people nowadays read books less than they did in the past? Why?
  • What factors make a book successful internationally?
